# Code of Conduct

Runqora is built for developers of every background and level of experience.
Everyone who takes part here, maintainers included, is expected to keep the
project welcoming, constructive and safe.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person, and make it actionable.
- Respect people's privacy, time and boundaries.
- Accept maintainers' decisions, and keep any disagreement calm and on topic.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised attention, doxxing, deliberate
disruption, and publishing anyone's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers, or through GitHub's
reporting tools on the comment, issue or pull request concerned. Reports are
handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock or close threads, issue
warnings, and temporarily or permanently restrict someone from taking part.

This code applies in all of the project's spaces, and when someone publicly
represents the project or its community.
